title: Account Deletion
---

<h1>Delete Your Account</h1>

<p>If you cannot delete your account in-app, you can request deletion of your Temper account and all associated data by emailing us at <a href="mailto:temperapp.support@gmail.com">temperapp.support@gmail.com</a>.</p>

<p>Include the email address associated with your Temper account in your request. We will process your request within 30 days.</p>

<h2>What gets deleted</h2>

<ul>
<li>Your account and login credentials</li>
<li>All workout logs and history</li>
<li>All training programs</li>
<li>Custom exercises</li>
<li>Account preferences</li>
</ul>

<h2>What is retained</h2>

<p>Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being purged as part of our regular backup rotation. No personally identifiable data is retained beyond that period.</p>
